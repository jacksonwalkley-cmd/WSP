      1-Rep Max

      Warm up thoroughly, then work up in confident singles to a new 1-rep max on each lift. Stop the moment a rep slows down or form breaks. That's the number to log, not the one after it. For pull-ups, do one set of max strict reps: full hang, chin over the bar, no kipping.
