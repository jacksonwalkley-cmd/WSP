      Alternate track

      High School Strength Program

      4 days/week · Squat x2, Bench x1, Pull-Ups x1 · Compound lifts only.

      Setting it up: Establish a Training Max (TM) for squat and bench — take each athlete's actual or best-estimated 1-rep max and use 90% of it as the TM. All percentages below are of TM, not true 1RM. For pull-ups, use max strict reps in one set as the baseline. The program runs as two 4-week waves (8 weeks total); retest or recalculate maxes after week 4's deload and repeat with the new numbers.

      Between Waves

      Test Week

      Retest

      After Week 4's deload, retest before starting the second wave. Same rules as any 1-rep max: warm up thoroughly, work up in confident singles, and stop the moment a rep slows down or form breaks — that's the number to log. For pull-ups, retest max strict reps in one set; that number decides which track you run next.

      Coaching note: Squatting heavy twice in the same week is the part most likely to wreck a high schooler's recovery if the Day 4 volume session isn't kept honestly light. Coach athletes to leave weight on the bar on Day 4 rather than chasing PRs there.
